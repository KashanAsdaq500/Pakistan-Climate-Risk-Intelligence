# National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) Pakistan — Heatwave Guidelines & Preparedness

**Authoritative Source:** National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), Prime Minister's Office, Islamabad, Pakistan.  
**Official Reference:** NDMA National Heatwave Management Guidelines, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and Heat Health Action Framework.

## National Heatwave SOPs and Directives

The National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), coordinating with Provincial Disaster Management Authorities (PDMA Punjab, PDMA Sindh, PDMA KP, PDMA Balochistan, and GBDMA), implements targeted risk-mitigation measures during peak summer cycles:

1. **Establishment of Heatstroke Relief Centers:**
   - Dedicated cooling stations equipped with oral rehydration salts (ORS), cold drinking water, shade structures, fans, and emergency first-aid paramedical staff in public transportation hubs, busy market squares, and municipal intersections.

2. **Hospital and Healthcare Readiness:**
   - Declaration of high alert across public and tertiary care hospitals.
   - Dedicated air-conditioned heatstroke stabilization wards, priority supply of IV fluids (Normal Saline and Ringer's Lactate), ice packs, and electrolyte management.
   - Emergency medical helpline connectivity (Rescue 1122 and district emergency cells).

3. **Public Advisory and Labor Protection:**
   - Regulating working hours for high-risk outdoor occupations (construction workers, agricultural laborers, street vendors, and traffic wardens) during the peak thermal window of 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
   - Awareness campaigns across electronic, digital, and print media regarding hydration and direct sun avoidance.

## Vulnerability Factors Identified by NDMA

NDMA identifies high-risk vulnerable groups across Pakistani urban and rural communities:
- **Elderly Citizens (Aged 65+) and Young Infants:** Possess reduced physiological thermoregulation and diminished thirst sensation.
- **Chronic Illness Patients:** Individuals suffering from cardiovascular disease, chronic kidney disease, hypertension, respiratory ailments, and diabetes.
- **Informal Labor Workforce:** Outdoor manual workers lacking access to shaded respite and potable water during peak radiation hours.
- **Densely Populated Informal Settlements:** Urban slums characterized by tin/metal roofing, lack of cross-ventilation, load-shedding vulnerability, and limited access to tap water.

## Community Heat Safety Protocols

NDMA standard advisory instructs citizens to:
- Drink abundant water regularly, even in the absence of explicit thirst, avoiding sugary or caffeinated beverages.
- Wear loose, light-colored, lightweight cotton clothing to reflect solar radiation.
- Avoid stepping outdoors between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM without head cover (umbrella, cap, wet towel).
- Never leave children or elderly individuals in unattended closed vehicles under any circumstances.
